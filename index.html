Extra Rules

Hay Roll Multiplier

When calculating Hay roll payouts in the roll table:

  • If Hay quantity is 5–9, apply a 1.5× multiplier.
  • If Hay quantity is 10+, apply a 2× multiplier.
  • Otherwise, apply 1×.

This multiplier affects the roll payout values shown in the roll table (profit per roll), not the asset purchase net values.

Equipment Bonuses

  • Tractors increase hay gain by +20% per tractor additively, up to 5 tractors (+100%).
  • Harvesters increase grain gain by +20% per harvester additively, up to 5 harvesters (+100%).

Options to Buy

Able to buy in bulk once any one player reaches these net-worth benchmarks (buy window offers 1x up to your unlocked tier): 2x hay/grain at $150,000 — 2x of anything plus 3x hay/grain at $250,000 — 5x hay/grain at $500,000. Tiers lock again if no one holds the benchmark.

Market Rules (host picks per room)

  • Seasons — prices start normal and walk up and down as the room harvests: each crop runs its own cycle sized by player count, peaking at +25%.
  • Rubber-band — individual leader/trailer prices are disabled; everyone sees the same room-wide costs.
  • Estate — adds a shared surcharge: crops/farms/equipment per owned unit past the starting grant, cattle per 2 head, all off room averages.
  • Balance — the crop everyone piles into gets dear (up to 2×), the ignored ones go cheap (down to half). Hay (base $15,000) only ever discounts, never rises above base.
  • Boom&Bust — each crop walks its own random path: every room harvest moves a crop ±1%, zero-sum across crops and capped at ±50%. Same walk on every device.
  • Economy — enables the host's economy numbers below (unticked = standard $50k loan cap, 10% interest, 20% min down).
  • Harvest — enables the host's harvest numbers below (unticked = standard hay tiers and equipment bonuses).

Market prices show in the Cost column and the buy window. Player-to-player trades are negotiated and never adjusted — the Buy/Sell to player buttons pop up the trade forms. All seven boxes mix and match.

Scenarios (one-tap presets in the Make Room popup)

  • Standard — the classic game: all boxes off, standard money and harvests.
  • Drought — harsh harvests plus tight expensive credit ($25k cap, 25% interest, 40% down).
  • Bull Market — Balance + Boom&Bust with standard money; the best crop rotates as the walks move.
  • Debt-Free — no loans: 100% down, cash only.

A scenario just fills the popup form — hand-tweaking anything after flips the badge to Custom.

Settings (host can change these during the game)

  • Economy numbers (need Economy ☑) — max loan $, loan interest %, minimum down-payment %.
  • Harvest numbers (need Harvest ☑) — hay bonus tiers (qty → ×mult) and equipment bonus +%/unit with unit cap.

Every rule and number has an ⓘ button explaining what it does. The host can open Room Settings in the Market row and save changes for everyone at any time.
